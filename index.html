Inspection limitations

This is a limited, non-invasive inspection of accessible components at the time and place stated. It is not a teardown, engineering analysis, safety certification, warranty or guarantee. Concealed, inaccessible, intermittent or disassembled-component defects may not be detected. Diagnostic trouble codes are observations, not proof of a failed component. Unless specifically documented, this inspection does not include fluid laboratory analysis, internal engine/transmission inspection, compression or leak-down testing, alignment measurement, title/lien verification, accident-history research or emissions testing. Road testing is performed only when lawful and reasonably safe. Findings describe conditions observed on the inspection date. Further evaluation by a qualified repair facility may be appropriate.
